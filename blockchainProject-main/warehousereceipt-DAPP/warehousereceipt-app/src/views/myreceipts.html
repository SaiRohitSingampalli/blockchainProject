About Digital Warehouse Receipts

This is decentralized Marketplace application where the Warehouse Receipts are traded and used for taking delivery from the warehouse. Only Accounts who have the warehouse access can create the receipts and assign it to the owner. Anyone who is on the platform can buy with the price posted by seller. There is an option to bid for a price which buyer is interested in and depending on the highest bid provided seller can change his price looking at the demand for his receipt in the open market. A receipt can be destroyed only by the warehouse after the delivery, provided the owner gives him the permission to destroy the receipt.
